# CFPB Customer Support Pack

This is an example domain pack, not part of the generic OpenRAG Forge kernel. It supplies CFPB public complaint and official guidance adapters, authority-aware prompts, safety policies and the versioned benchmark used by the original OpenSupport project.

The pack deliberately keeps consumer narratives separate from official guidance. Complaint narratives are examples of consumer claims, not verified findings or legal conclusions.

The pack can be replaced by a company's own documents and policies without changing the core parser, Recipe, Trace or Evidence Capsule contracts.
